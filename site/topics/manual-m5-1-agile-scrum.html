5.1 Agile & Scrum Framework: Roles, Ceremonies & Artifacts

1. The Base: What is Agile vs Scrum and Why Do They Exist?

Agile is an iterative software mindset (4 Values, 12 Principles) that delivers working software in small frequent increments with continuous customer feedback. Scrum is the most popular lightweight Agile framework that organizes work into fixed time-boxes called Sprints (typically 2 weeks) using 3 Roles, 5 Events (Ceremonies), and 3 Artifacts.

The Problem They Solve (In Plain Words): Traditional Waterfall locked requirements for 9 months before showing anything to the customer—so when market needs changed in Month 4, the final product was obsolete on launch day, and QA had to cram 9 months of testing into the last 2 weeks. Scrum delivers a tested, potentially shippable product increment every 2 weeks.

2. The 3 Scrum Roles (Accountabilities)

  1. 1. Product Owner (PO): Owns the WHAT and WHY—manages and prioritizes the Product Backlog, defines User Stories & Acceptance Criteria, and represents business/customer ROI.
  2. 2. Scrum Master (SM): Owns the Scrum Process—a servant-leader who coaches the team on Agile, facilitates ceremonies, shields the team from mid-sprint scope creep, and removes blockers/impediments.
  3. 3. Developers (Cross-Functional Delivery Team — Devs + Manual QA + SDETs): Owns the HOW—self-organizing engineers who design, code, test, automate, and deliver a "Done" Increment every Sprint. *(Scrum recognizes no sub-titles inside the delivery team: QA/SDET is an equal Developer accountability.)*

PurposeInspect: What went well? What didn't? What 1 action item will we improve next Sprint?

4. The 3 Scrum Artifacts & Their Commitments

Scrum ArtifactCommitmentWhat It Contains
1. Product BacklogProduct GoalSingle prioritized ordered list of all features, bugs & tech debt owned by the PO
2. Sprint BacklogSprint GoalSelected User Stories + action plan committed by the Developers/QA for the current Sprint
3. Product IncrementDefinition of Done (DoD)Sum of all completed, tested, integrated stories meeting quality standards at Sprint end

Interview Answer: "Scrum has 3 Roles (Product Owner, Scrum Master, and cross-functional Developers including QA/SDET), 5 Events (The Sprint container, Sprint Planning, 15-min Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective), and 3 Artifacts (Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, and Increment—each tied to Product Goal, Sprint Goal, and Definition of Done)."
