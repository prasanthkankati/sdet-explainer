Interview

M5.1 Question Bank: Agile & Scrum Roles, Ceremonies & Artifacts (35 Questions)

Tier 1–5 Complete Question Bank (Q1–Q35)

  1. Q1: What are the 4 core values of the Agile Manifesto?
    A: (1) Individuals & interactions over processes/tools, (2) Working software over comprehensive documentation, (3) Customer collaboration over contract negotiation, (4) Responding to change over following a plan.
  2. Q2: What are the 3 Roles, 5 Events, and 3 Artifacts in Scrum?
    A: Roles: PO, Scrum Master, Developers (including QA). Events: Sprint, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective. Artifacts: Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment.
  3. Q3: What is the difference between a Product Owner and a Scrum Master?
    A: PO owns the product value & backlog priority; SM owns the Scrum process & impediment removal.
  4. Q4: What is the time-box for Daily Scrum, and who is allowed to speak?
    A: Strictly 15 minutes. Only the Developers (Devs + QA) actively participate to inspect Sprint Goal progress; PO/Managers may listen silently.
  5. Q5: What is the difference between Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective?
    A: Sprint Review inspects the Product (live demo to stakeholders); Sprint Retrospective inspects the Team & Process (internal reflection on how to work better).
  6. Q6: How does QA work inside an Agile Sprint ("In-Sprint Automation") vs "N-1 Automation"?
    A: In In-Sprint Automation, QA writes tests during Refinement, tests manually and automates Selenium/API scripts inside the same sprint before marking the story `Done`. In N-1 Automation, stories are tested manually in Sprint N and automated in Sprint N+1 (anti-pattern for regression lag, though used when UI locators are unstable).
  7. Q7: What if a Developer hands over 5 stories to QA on Day 9 of a 10-day Sprint ("Mini-Waterfall / Water-Scrum-Fall")?
    A: Flag it in Standup & Retrospective! Fix via smaller story slicing, Shift-Left API contract testing while UI is built, and setting a WIP (Work-In-Progress) limit in Jira.
  8. Q8: Can a Product Owner add new stories mid-Sprint?
    A: No—the Sprint Backlog is protected once Planning ends; urgent scope changes require negotiating a swap or cancelling the Sprint (rare).
  9. Q9: What is a Spike in Scrum?
    A: A time-boxed research/prototyping task (e.g., investigating Playwright vs Selenium for a shadow-DOM widget) when effort cannot yet be estimated.
  10. Q10: What happens to unfinished User Stories at the end of a Sprint?
    A: They are NOT counted in Velocity; they return to the Product Backlog for the PO to re-prioritize in the next Sprint Planning.
  11. Q11–Q35 Quick Drills: (Q11) Who prioritizes Product Backlog? PO. (Q12) Who assigns tasks inside Sprint Backlog? Self-organizing Developers/QA (not SM/PO). (Q13) What is Backlog Refinement? Clarifying upcoming stories & Acceptance Criteria before Planning. (Q14) Is Scrum Master a people manager? No, servant-leader. (Q15) Recommended Scrum team size? ≤ 10 people. (Q16) Three pillars of Empiricism? Transparency, Inspection, Adaptation. (Q17) What is Sprint Goal? Single objective giving coherence to the Sprint. (Q18) When does Sprint `N+1` start? Immediately after Sprint `N` Retro ends. (Q19–Q35: Scaling Scrum [SAFe / LeSS / Scrum of Scrums], QA pairing with Devs on Unit/API tests [Three Amigos: PO + Dev + QA], Smoke/Regression automation in CI/CD before Sprint Review demo, handling Production P1 bugs via Sprint capacity buffer, and why QA never demos untested code in Sprint Review).
